How it works

Get your item back in 4 simple steps

No more searching WhatsApp groups. Everything happens in one place.

1

Create an account

Register once and login securely with your email and password.

2

Post your item

Lost or found, add a photo, the place, and where the item is kept.

3

Search and match

Filter by category, location or any keyword to spot your item fast.

4

Get it back

Contact the poster, collect your item, and mark the post as returned.

What it does

Everything a campus Lost & Found needs

Simple tools that make lost items easy to report and easy to find.

📸

Post with a photo

Add a clear picture so owners can recognise their item right away.

📍

Where found, where kept

Every found item shows the place it was found and where it is kept safely.

🔍

Smart search and filters

Search by name, place or category. Capital or small letters do not matter.

🔐

Secure login

Passwords are stored in a protected form and every post is linked to its owner.

📞

Contact details

Posters can share a phone number or email so the owner can reach them.

✅

Mark as returned

Only the owner of a post can mark it returned, edit it, or delete it.
